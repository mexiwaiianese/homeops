# portonOS workspace pricing

Internal reference for published manager plans. Customer-facing copy lives in `lib/public-site.ts`.

The workspace is billed once a year. The monthly rates below are how that annual invoice is calculated.

| Plan | Annual bill | How the year is priced | Properties included | ACH on included properties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core | $966 the first year, then $1,188 a year | 3 months at $25 and 9 months at $99 in year one | 25 | Free |
| Operations | $1,788 a year | $149/mo | 75 | Free |
| Portfolio | $3,588 a year | $299/mo | 250 | Free |

Each property past the included total is $18 a year ($1.50 a month times 12). Add one before the renewal date and that $18 is prorated to the renewal date, including a partial month. Eleven months left is $16.50. About two weeks left is about $0.75. The payment provider's transaction fee is charged in addition to that amount. At the next renewal, each extra property is $18 for the full year. There is no cliff and no forced tier upgrade.

The only added workspace charge is that property overage, plus the transaction fee on the overage payment. There is no onboarding fee and no per-lead charge. ACH stays free on included properties at every tier.

Applicant screening is on Operations and Portfolio. The credit, criminal, and eviction report is not part of the annual bill. Each adult pays the screening partner's price, passed through. The manager can pay that price or waive it. portonOS does not publish a markup. Competitor prices on the pricing page are their published rates as of October 2026.
